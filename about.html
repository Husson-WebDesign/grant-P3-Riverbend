Our Academy

Riverbend Music Academy was created to provide professional private music instruction in an environment where students feel comfortable learning. The academy serves students with different musical goals and levels of experience. Some students may be preparing for performances or auditions, while others simply want to learn an instrument for personal enjoyment.

Why Riverbend exists

Riverbend Music Academy was established around a simple belief: music education should challenge students while helping them enjoy the process of learning. It should be structured and professional, and it should also be encouraging, approachable, and enjoyable.

Daniel Mercer, the academy's founder and director, has taught private piano lessons for more than fifteen years, working with students from young beginners to advanced adult musicians. Riverbend was created to give students professional instruction in a place where they feel comfortable learning.

Some of our students are preparing for performances or auditions. Others simply want to learn an instrument for the joy of it. Riverbend is built for both.

The kind of place we want Riverbend to be

We want students to feel comfortable asking questions, trying new things, and taking the time they need. Every student learns at a different speed, so our instructors set clear expectations and adapt their teaching when a student needs a different approach.

Most lessons at Riverbend are individual private lessons, which keeps the attention on each student's own goals. Progress is recognized along the way, and we encourage students to focus on steady improvement rather than comparing themselves with other musicians.

Supporting beginners

The academy welcomes students who have never taken a music lesson. You do not need to arrive knowing how to:

  • Read music
  • Hold an instrument correctly
  • Understand musical terminology
  • Perform in front of others

Those skills are developed during lessons, and students can ask questions and build confidence gradually. Beginners of every age are welcome, including adults learning an instrument for the first time and adults returning to music after several years.

Many young beginners start with 30-minute lessons, and we will help you choose the right length. For new students, short, regular practice sessions usually work better than one long session right before a lesson.

Room to grow

Students with more experience continue to develop at Riverbend through more advanced repertoire, technique, performance preparation, and musical expression. If you are working toward an audition, a school performance, or a recital, tell your instructor. Lessons can be shaped around that goal.
