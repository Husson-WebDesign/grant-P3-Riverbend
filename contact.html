Lesson inquiries

Students or parents interested in lessons should contact Riverbend Music Academy by phone or email. When making an inquiry, please be prepared to provide:

  • Student name
  • Age group
  • Instrument of interest
  • Current experience level
  • Preferred lesson day
  • General scheduling availability

The academy will recommend an instructor and lesson length based on availability and student needs.
