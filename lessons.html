Our Lessons

Piano

Ages 5+ Beginner–Advanced 30/45/60 min

Piano lessons introduce students to keyboard technique, music reading, rhythm, coordination, scales, chords, and musical interpretation. Beginning students build foundational skills while more experienced students may focus on advanced repertoire, technique, performance preparation, and musical expression. Students should have regular access to an acoustic piano or suitable digital keyboard for practice.

Guitar

Ages 8+ Beginner–Advanced 30/45/60 min

Guitar instruction is available for acoustic and electric guitar. Students may study:

  • Chords
  • Scales
  • Rhythm
  • Music Reading
  • Tablature
  • Technique
  • Improvisation
  • Selected Repertoire

Lessons may include contemporary, rock, folk, acoustic, or traditional material depending on student goals and instructor recommendations.

Voice

Ages 10+ Beginner–Advanced 30/45/60 min

Voice lessons focus on healthy singing habits and musical development. Topics may include:

  • Breathing
  • Posture
  • Pitch
  • Tone
  • Diction
  • Vocal Control
  • Interpretation
  • Performance Confidence

Repertoire may be selected according to age, vocal development, experience, and musical interests.

Drums

Ages 8+ Beginner–Advanced 30/45 min

Drum lessons focus on rhythm, coordination, timing, technique, reading, and musical groove. Students may work with:

  • Drum Set
  • Practice Pad
  • Rhythmic Exercises
  • Basic Music Notation
  • Performance Patterns

Beginning students are introduced to proper stick technique and rhythmic fundamentals before progressing to more complex material.

Violin

Ages 6+ Beginner–Advanced 30/45/60 min

Violin instruction develops:

  • Proper Posture
  • Instrument Position
  • Bow Technique
  • Intonation
  • Rhythm
  • Music Reading
  • Scales
  • Repertoire

Beginning students develop fundamental habits while more experienced students work toward greater technical control and musical expression.
