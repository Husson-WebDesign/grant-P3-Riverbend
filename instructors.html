Our Instructors

Riverbend Music Academy was created to provide professional private music instruction in an environment where students feel comfortable learning. The academy serves students with different musical goals and levels of experience. Some students may be preparing for performances or auditions, while others simply want to learn an instrument for personal enjoyment.

Daniel Mercer

**Position:** Academy Director and Piano Instructor **Instrument:** Piano Daniel Mercer is the founder and director of Riverbend Music Academy. He has taught private piano lessons for more than fifteen years and has worked with students ranging from young beginners to advanced adult musicians. His instruction combines technique, music reading, rhythm, repertoire, and musical interpretation. Daniel also works with students preparing for performances and auditions. ### Teaching Philosophy > Strong fundamentals should give students greater freedom to enjoy music. Daniel believes students make lasting progress when they understand both how to perform music and why particular musical techniques are important.

Elena Park

**Position:** Piano and Violin Instructor **Instruments:** Piano and Violin Elena Park teaches both piano and violin. She has experience working with children, teenagers, and adult learners. Her lessons emphasize technical development, careful listening, music reading, and gradual improvement. Elena particularly enjoys helping students who are beginning an instrument for the first time. ### Teaching Philosophy > Students make the greatest progress when expectations are clear and improvement is recognized. Elena believes that encouragement and structure should work together. Students should understand what they are expected to practice while also recognizing the progress they have already made.

Claire Donovan

**Position:** Voice Instructor **Instrument:** Voice Claire Donovan teaches healthy vocal technique, musical interpretation, and performance confidence. She works with students at different levels of experience and helps singers understand how breathing, posture, tone, and musical expression work together. ### Teaching Philosophy > Every singer should understand how to use their voice safely and expressively. Claire emphasizes healthy technique and age-appropriate repertoire.
