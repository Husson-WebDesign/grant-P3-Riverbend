# Client Feedback: Riverbend Music Academy Website

**Website:** https://husson-webdesign.github.io/grant-P3-Riverbend/

Hi Grant,

Thank you for sharing the first version of our website. I can see that you have put thought into presenting Riverbend Music Academy. The blue and gold colors give the site a professional feel, and I like that visitors can already explore our lessons, learn about our instructors, and find our contact information.

For the next version, I would like the website to feel warmer, easier to explore, and more useful to someone who is considering music lessons for the first time. Below are my requests, organized by the stages of the redesign.

## Phase 1: Clarify Our Message and Content

### 1. Make the homepage more inviting

The current introduction explains our teaching philosophy, but I would like visitors to understand immediately that we offer music lessons in Bangor, Maine.

Please create a shorter, more welcoming main message. It should speak to children, adults, beginners, and experienced students. For example:

> **Find Your Sound at Riverbend**  
> Music lessons for every age, every skill level, and every musical journey.

You are welcome to develop your own wording. I would also like visitors to have two clear choices: explore our lessons or meet our instructors.

### 2. Help visitors understand how to get started

A parent visiting our website for the first time may not know what happens next. Please add a simple section that explains the process:

1. Explore the instruments we teach.
2. Learn about our instructors.
3. Prepare a lesson inquiry.

Please make this section easy to scan and visually appealing.

### 3. Make the About page feel more personal

The current page explains our teaching approach, but I would like it to tell more of a story about the academy. Please help visitors understand:

- Why Riverbend exists.
- What kind of learning environment we want to create.
- How we support beginners.
- How students with more experience can continue to grow.

Please use the information available in the project. If you need a detail that has not been provided, identify it as a question for me rather than making it up.

### 4. Clarify who teaches each instrument

The Lessons page includes piano, guitar, voice, drums, and violin. I can see instructors associated with piano, violin, and voice, but I could not tell who teaches guitar or drums.

Please review the information and make this clearer. If the instructor information for a lesson is not available, indicate that instructor details are not yet available rather than inventing a name.

## Phase 2: Improve the Look and Feel

### 5. Create a more polished and consistent appearance

I like the blue and gold colors and would like to keep them. Please refine the design so that all pages feel like part of the same academy. In particular, I would like:

- Consistent headings, buttons, and spacing.
- Sections that are easy to distinguish.
- Comfortable paragraph lengths.
- Lesson and instructor information presented in attractive, consistent cards.
- A layout that does not feel crowded or unfinished.

The finished website should feel professional while still being warm and welcoming to families.

### 6. Choose images that reflect our academy

The homepage image creates a musical atmosphere. For the redesign, I would also like to see images that help visitors picture the type of experience we offer, such as individual music lessons, students practicing, or the instruments available.

Please make sure images look clear, fit their spaces properly, and can be used for the project.

### 7. Give each page a clear next step

When someone finishes reading a page, I do not want them wondering where to go next. For example:

- The About page could lead to our instructors.
- An instructor profile could lead to the lessons they teach.
- A lesson description could lead to the lesson-interest form.

## Phase 3: Make the Website Easy to Use on Any Device

### 8. Improve the navigation

The current navigation is straightforward, and I would like to keep the same main page choices. Please make it easy for visitors to tell which page they are on and return to the homepage by selecting our logo.

On a phone, the menu should be easy to open, read, and close.

### 9. Make the site comfortable to use on a phone

Many parents may look at the site while using their phones. Please make sure:

- Text is easy to read without zooming.
- Images fit the screen.
- Buttons and links are easy to tap.
- Lesson and instructor cards rearrange neatly.
- Visitors do not need to scroll sideways.

Please test the site at several screen sizes before presenting the redesign.

## Phase 4: Improve the Lesson and Contact Experience

### 10. Make it easier to compare lessons

I appreciate the detail already provided on the Lessons page, including the recommended ages and lesson lengths. Please make the five offerings easier to compare at a glance.

For each instrument, I would like visitors to see:

- The instrument name and an appropriate image.
- The recommended age.
- The experience levels supported.
- The available lesson lengths.
- A short description of what students may learn.

Visitors should then be able to explore more details or prepare an inquiry.

### 11. Help visitors find the right lesson

Please add a simple way for visitors to find the instrument they are interested in. For example, they could select an instrument from a list or type its name into a search box.

If no lesson matches their search, please show a friendly message and give them a way to see all lessons again.

### 12. Make the lesson-interest form clear

I like that the current form asks about the student's age, instrument, experience, and availability. Those are the kinds of details that would help us understand a student's needs.

For this class project, I understand that the form will be a **demonstration only** and will not send or save information. Please make that clear before someone begins filling it out.

After a visitor completes the form, I would like them to see a neatly presented preview of their answers. They should be able to go back and make changes. The page should not say that an inquiry was sent.

### 13. Update the lesson buttons

The current lesson buttons say “Enroll Now,” but they lead to the Contact page. Please change the wording so visitors understand that they are preparing an inquiry rather than enrolling. For example:

> **Ask About Piano Lessons**

If possible, when someone selects a lesson, have that instrument already selected on the interest form. They should still be able to change their choice.

## Phase 5: Make Information Easier to Maintain

### 14. Organize lesson information for future updates

As the academy owner, I would like the website to be easier to update if we later change a lesson description, age recommendation, or lesson length.

Please organize the lesson information so that it is kept together in a separate **JSON file** and used to display the lessons on the website. I do not need to understand how the file works, but I would like the project documentation to explain where the information is located and how a future developer can update it.

For this stage, please make sure:

- All five instruments are included.
- The information displayed on the website matches the information in the file.
- The lesson search or filter uses that information.
- Visitors see a helpful message if the lesson information cannot be displayed.

If you have time, you may use the same approach for instructor profiles.

## Phase 6: Final Review and Handoff

### 15. Check the website carefully

Before presenting the redesigned site, please review it as though it were ready for a real client to see. Make sure:

- All pages, links, images, and buttons work.
- The site is easy to navigate with a keyboard.
- Text is readable against its background.
- Images include descriptions where appropriate.
- The website works on different screen sizes.
- The demonstration form clearly explains its limitations.
- The lesson information displays correctly on GitHub Pages.
- Spelling and business information are consistent throughout the site.

### 16. Provide a short handoff

When the redesign is complete, please provide:

- The link to the published website.
- The link to the project repository.
- A short explanation of the changes you made.
- Screenshots showing the site on a desktop and a phone.
- A list of any information you still need from me.
- Instructions for a future developer explaining where the lesson information is kept and how to update it.

## My Priorities

| Priority | What I would like improved |
| --- | --- |
| **First** | Make the homepage message clear and inviting. |
| **First** | Clearly identify the contact form as a demonstration. |
| **First** | Make the site easy to use on a phone. |
| **Next** | Improve the lesson cards, instructor information, and page-to-page journey. |
| **Next** | Add lesson search or filtering and organize lesson information in JSON. |
| **Before handoff** | Test accessibility, links, images, and the published website. |

My goal is for a parent or prospective student to visit the site, feel welcomed, understand what Riverbend offers, and easily decide what they would like to explore next.
