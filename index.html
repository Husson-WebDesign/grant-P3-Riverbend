Our Academy

Riverbend Music Academy was created to provide professional private music instruction in an environment where students feel comfortable learning. Some students may be preparing for performances or auditions, while others simply want to learn an instrument for personal enjoyment.

Appointments

Lessons are scheduled by appointment. Students or parents interested in lessons should contact Riverbend Music Academy by phone or email. When making an inquiry, prospective students should be prepared to provide:

  • Student Name
  • Age Group
  • Instrument of Interest
  • Current Experience Level
  • Preferred Lesson Day
  • General Scheduling Availability

The academy will recommend an instructor and lesson length based on availability and student needs.
