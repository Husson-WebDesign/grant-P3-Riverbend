Music lessons should challenge students without taking away the enjoyment of making music.

Riverbend Music Academy provides supportive private instruction for beginning through advanced students in a welcoming environment. Whether a student is picking up an instrument for the first time, preparing for a performance, or returning to music after several years, our instructors work to develop strong musical foundations while helping students pursue their individual goals.

Our Academy

Riverbend Music Academy was created to provide professional private music instruction in an environment where students feel comfortable learning. The academy serves students with different musical goals and levels of experience. Some students may be preparing for performances or auditions, while others simply want to learn an instrument for personal enjoyment.

Our Approach to Teaching

Riverbend Music Academy believes that strong musical fundamentals provide the foundation for long-term growth.
Lessons may include:

  • Technique
  • Music Reading
  • Rhythm
  • Listening
  • Repertoire
  • Performance Skills
  • Practice Strategies

Supporting Beginners

The academy welcomes students who have never taken a music lesson.
Beginners are not expected to arrive already knowing how to:

  • Read Music
  • Hold an Instrument Correctly
  • Understand Musical Terminology
  • Perform in Front of Others
These skills are developed during lessons. The goal is to create a learning environment where students can ask questions and build confidence gradually.

Student Development

Progress in music depends on:

  • Regular Attendance
  • Consistent Practice
  • Appropriate Instruction
  • Realistic Goals
  • Patience
The academy encourages students to focus on steady improvement rather than comparing their progress with other musicians.

Our Lessons

Piano

Recommended Ages: 5 through adult Experience Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced Lesson Lengths: 30, 45, or 60 minutes
Piano lessons introduce students to keyboard technique, music reading, rhythm, coordination, scales, chords, and musical interpretation. Beginning students build foundational skills while more experienced students may focus on advanced repertoire, technique, performance preparation, and musical expression. Students should have regular access to an acoustic piano or suitable digital keyboard for practice.

Guitar

Recommended Ages: 8 through adult Experience Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced Lesson Lengths: 30, 45, or 60 minutes
Guitar instruction is available for acoustic and electric guitar. Students may study:

  • Chords
  • Scales
  • Rhythm
  • Music Reading
  • Tablature
  • Technique
  • Improvisation
  • Selected Repertoire

Lessons may include contemporary, rock, folk, acoustic, or traditional material depending on student goals and instructor recommendations.

Voice

Recommended Ages: 10 through adult Experience Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced Lesson Lengths: 30, 45, or 60 minutes
Voice lessons focus on healthy singing habits and musical development. Topics may include:

  • Breathing
  • Posture
  • Pitch
  • Tone
  • Diction
  • Vocal Control
  • Interpretation
  • Performance Confidence

Repertoire may be selected according to age, vocal development, experience, and musical interests.

Drums

Recommended Ages: 8 through adult Experience Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced Lesson Lengths: 30 or 45 minutes
Drum lessons focus on rhythm, coordination, timing, technique, reading, and musical groove. Students may work with:

  • Drum Set
  • Practice Pad
  • Rhythmic Exercises
  • Basic Music Notation
  • Performance Patterns

Beginning students are introduced to proper stick technique and rhythmic fundamentals before progressing to more complex material.

Violin

Recommended Ages: 6 through adult Experience Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced Lesson Lengths: 30, 45, or 60 minutes
Violin instruction develops:

  • Proper Posture
  • Instrument Position
  • Bow Technique
  • Intonation
  • Rhythm
  • Music Reading
  • Scales
  • Repertoire

Beginning students develop fundamental habits while more experienced students work toward greater technical control and musical expression.
